The Computer Vision Pipeline

How the Tilapia Counter Works

From overhead 4K optical capture in the controlled channel to spatial tracking and academic benchmarking in four streamlined stages.

01

1. Optical Acquisition

Overhead 4K UHD camera mounted perpendicularly on a tripod captures live video of fingerlings in the 60L blue tub under controlled circular ring lighting.

Input: 4K UHD Feed (60L Channel)
02

2. Deep Detection

Standardized 640×640 frames processed through YOLOv8, YOLOv9, or YOLOv10n neural networks detecting juvenile Nile Tilapia with bounding boxes and confidence.

Model: YOLOv8 / YOLOv9 / YOLOv10n
03

3. Tracking & Gate

Persistent spatial trajectory IDs trace moving fingerlings continuously. Directional virtual tripwires count in/out passages with zero recounts.

Logic: Spatial IDs + Tripwire Gate
04

4. Analytics & WAL

Outputs feed SQLite Write-Ahead Logging (WAL), computing real-time stocking densities, BFAR size dispersal logs, and thesis metrics (Precision, Recall, F1, MAE, MAPE).

Output: SQLite WAL DB + CSV Export
